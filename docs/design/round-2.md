# UI design, round 2: direction A "Quiet signal"

Status: **tokens and components** (redesign slice B, 2026-10-23, `docs/tech_doc.md` §40), **shell, page
structures and the IA step** (slice C, 2026-10-28, §41) and **motion, view transitions and the console frames**
(slice D, 2026-10-29, §43) implemented. The Go to palette, its `/go` page and the shortcuts (plan 04 §11 item 7, deferred
out of slice D) are implemented too (2026-10-30, §52; §4b below).

Source: the round-2 mockup package (`PACKAGE.md`, `a-quiet-signal/`) in the design worktree. The maintainer chose A
on 2026-10-22, borrowing C's shared-axis flight recorder and B's incident mode for later slices.

## 1. Concept

A calm instrument that only raises its voice where something is wrong. Healthy is quiet (plain text and a
green mark, no fills); problems are loud and specific. Status is carried by **fill weight**, a glyph and a
word, never by colour alone:

| State | Treatment |
|---|---|
| OK | text colour, green mark icon (circle-check), no fill |
| Warning | amber tint, amber mark outline (triangle-alert) |
| Critical | solid critical fill, inverse text (octagon-x) |
| Powered off | solid slate fill, inverse text (power-off) |
| Stale / no data | tinted, **dashed** outline (clock) |
| Info, maintenance | blue tint and outline (info, wrench) |
| Unknown | dashed neutral outline, muted text (circle-question-mark) |

Rules (all enforced by `TestThemeContrast` and `theme_hue_test.go`, every palette and mode): text and inks
≥ 4.5:1 (7:1 for text in High contrast) on every surface and tint; marks, field borders and the focus ring
≥ 3:1; the primary button 7:1, hovered too; the warning mark ≥ 0.12 in OKLab lightness from the critical fill and ≥ 0.1 from the critical mark; the focus ring
distinct from the warning colours. The complete resolved table is `internal/web/ui/testdata/theme-hex.txt`.

Scale: spacing `--sp-1…8` (4 px base); radii `--r-badge` 6 px, `--r-ctl` 8 px, `--r-panel` 12 px, `--r-pill`;
type `--fs-0` .75rem (eyebrow), `--fs-1` .8125rem (meta), `--fs-2` .875rem, `--fs-3` 1rem, `--fs-h3`,
`--fs-4`, `--fs-5` (h1, fluid to 2rem); Inter for the UI with tabular slashed-zero numerals, JetBrains Mono for
IDs, values and code.

## 3. Components (slice B)

- **Type**: h1 600 on `--fs-5`; h2 sentence case at `--fs-h3`; eyebrow labels (uppercase, `--fs-0`, tracked)
  only for strip and list group labels and the sidebar heading.
- **Links** are the accent, underlined; the **accent is never a fill that could read as a state**: primary
  buttons are neutral ink, the accent marks links, the current item (tint + 3 px inset bar) and chosen cards.
- **Badges** (`health` partial, `.badge.b-*`): icon + word at `--fs-1` 600, fill weight as in §1. **Chips**
  are pills; the age chip shows Live / Aging (amber tint) / Stale (dashed).
- **Buttons** `.btn` (neutral), `.btn-secondary`, `.btn-quiet`, `.btn-danger` (solid critical),
  `.btn-danger-outline`, `.btn-caution` (amber outline), disabled (dashed, muted, with its `why-off` text).
- **Forms**: fields on `--surface-2` with a 3:1 border; invalid fields a 2 px critical mark border plus the
  message; radio/checkbox cards chosen = accent border and tint.
- **Panels**: `--r-panel`, hairline, soft shadow in light mode; status panels a 3 px inset mark stripe.
  **Alerts**: tinted, full mark border (stale dashed).
- **Tables**: muted sentence-case headers, hairline rows, hover tint, row states as tint + inset mark;
  stacked labelled rows on phones. **Headroom meter**: rounded track, mark fill, warning and critical ticks,
  the headroom always also in text.
- **Icons**: Lucide 1.53.0 in one in-page sprite, 24 grid, stroke 1.8, `currentColor`, always decorative next
  to a visible word; live data uses the activity line, stale the clock. **Brand**: the side-channel mark;
  five status favicons from the fleet's worst state, whose badge differs in shape (dot, triangle, octagon,
  ring, square) as well as colour.

## 4. Shell and pages (slice C)

Stylesheets: `tokens.css` → `themes.css` → `app.css` (components) → `shell.css` (shell) → `pages.css`
(page layouts) → `motion.css` (slice D, all motion); none defines a colour.

- **Top bar** 3.5 rem, opaque `--surface`: the mark, the wordmark (`oob` mono, a dot in the fleet's worst
  state, `manager`), the fleet pill (fill weight as the badges; on phones only its short worst word), the
  clock with the data age, and the account menu (avatar initial, Theme, Go to…, Display preferences, Help, About and
  licenses, Sign out). Below 1024 px the account menu and the sidebar move into the **Menu sheet**.
- **Sidebar** 16.5 rem on `--bg`, dimmed: Overview, Servers, Firmware, Events, Activity, Settings, Help; then
  every server as a status tile, its name and a **sub-line in words** ("47 °C", "Powered off", "Stale ·
  9 min", "Maintenance · 41 °C"); current item = accent tint + 3 px inset bar.
- **Server header** on every tab: name and tags, identity line, state chips (power, health, data age), and
  the action menus **Console ▾ · Power ▾ · More ▾** (`<details>`; items with an icon, label and sub-line;
  unavailable items stay with their reason; separators between groups; danger items in the critical ink).
  On phones the menus move to a **sticky bottom bar**. Viewers see "Actions need the operator role."
- **Tabs**: an accent ink underline (its own `.tab-ink` element, so it can slide; slice D); on phones a 4-column segmented grid with icons. Sub-navigation (log
  kind, chart range) is a **segmented control**. Breadcrumbs are an ordered list with `/` separators.
- **Overview**: the **verdict-first hero** (eyebrow, verdict as the h1, the worst item with its badge,
  counts, draw, events, data age, auto-refresh, "Also needs attention"); **incident mode** while a server is
  critical (display type, the critical count as a display numeral, one static glow: a blurred shadow of the
  critical fill); then the **flight recorder** (wide screens) or the compact list (phones, or no history),
  with In maintenance and Events in the rail.
- **Security tab**: the posture map (one cell per check: fail solid, warning tinted, unreadable dashed,
  accepted outlined, info a dot, pass a low bar) as the group jump list; passes and infos fold into "N
  more checks".
- **Alerts** lead with their state icon; **disclosures** lead with a chevron that turns when open;
  **steppers** draw the check icon; the **pager** is outlined controls.

## 4a. Motion and consoles (slice D)

- **Budget** (plan 04 §11 item 5): at most 250 ms (`--dur-1` 120 ms, `--dur-2` 200 ms), ease-out
  (`--ease`). Everything that moves is in `motion.css`, inside `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`; under
  reduced motion nothing moves. Nothing moves on alarms or status changes.
- **Cross-document view transitions** (item 6): `@view-transition { navigation: auto }`, a 200 ms crossfade; the
  top bar and sidebar are named so they stay put, the **server head morphs** between tabs and the **tab ink
  slides** to the new tab. Reloads and the auto-refresh do not transition. Chromium and Safari animate; Firefox
  navigates instantly.
- **Micro-interactions kept**: menu and Menu-sheet entrances (fade and 6 px drop, `@starting-style`), disclosure
  bodies fading in, colour fades on hover and focus, chevrons turning, and the **value-change tint**: a
  reading on the Sensors tab that differs from the previous poll is tinted with `--accent-bg`, holds 600 ms
  (`--hold`, a delay) and fades in 200 ms. Not kept: the hover lift and the drawn check (not chosen in item 5).
- **Consoles** (item 13, restyled only): one frame per console, a toolbar strip on `--surface` over the
  always-black stage, radius `--r-panel`; the connection state is a chip (dashed while connecting, a green
  outline and mark when connected, a red tint on an error); the transport line is a chip with a lock. The
  session panels stay beside it; the layout and every control are unchanged.

## 4b. Go to palette and shortcuts (plan 04 §11 item 7)

- **Go to** lists every page, server and setting the user can open (exactly what the sidebar and the account menu
  show them), in groups: Pages, Servers, Settings (admins), Account; the palette adds **This server** (the tabs of
  the server page it is on) and **Recent** (the last six pages of this browser tab). Without scripts it is the `/go`
  page, with a filter field and the shortcut table; the account menu and the Menu sheet link to it ("Go to…").
- **Palette**: a modal dialog near the top of the viewport (`--surface`, `--r-panel`, `--elev-2`, over a `--scrim`
  backdrop): a search field with the search icon and a close button, the grouped list (eyebrow group headings, an
  option per row with its label and a muted hint; the active option is the sidebar's current-item treatment: an
  `--accent-bg` tint with a 3 px accent bar), and a key legend with the link to `/go`. It enters like a menu (fade and
  6 px drop, 200 ms) and not at all under reduced motion.
- **Keys**: Ctrl+K or ⌘K opens it; `/` jumps to the page's filter field (or opens the palette where there is none);
  `g o` Overview, `g s` Servers; `?` the shortcut list. Never while typing in a field, and never on a page with a
  connected console. The single-key shortcuts can be switched off per browser on `/go`.

## 5. Deviations from the mockup, and why

- One ink per status for text plus a separate **mark** for glyphs, outlines and meter fills (the mockup has
  the same split as "X" and "ink"); the app keeps its existing semantic names (`--ok`, `--ok-bg`) and adds
  `-mark`, so no template changed for colour.
- The mockup's translucent hairlines (`color-mix` of the text) are opaque `--border` values: the contrast test
  must evaluate plain colours.
- Compact 36 px table rows stay the default (§11 item 4); the mockup's rows are taller.
- No glass on the bars: a translucent bar needs colour arithmetic the contrast test cannot evaluate; the bars
  are opaque, which is the mockup's own fallback under reduced transparency.
- Confirm pages keep "what happens" before the form (the mockup puts the form first): the reading order
  stays consequences, then decision, and Cancel keeps the default focus (audit R2-05).
- The Overview recorder draws one trace per server (its hottest sensor) and marks stretches without
  readings; the mockup's power and health bands need a state history the app does not store. Its "since
  your last visit" shading and the Now / Since-your-last-visit rail are not built (no per-user visit record).
- Server Overview keeps the Status panel (with its sparklines) instead of the mockup's vitals tiles, and the
  console cards stay on it (the native iDRAC console launch is a form, not a menu link).
- Fan boost is its own page (More ▾ → Fan boost…), carrying the existing lease card.
- Slice D: no main-column view-transition name (a named column would stretch between pages of different
  heights; the root crossfade covers it). The value-change tint has no static ▲/▼ glyph under reduced motion:
  the maintainer's decision is "all off under reduced motion". The consoles keep the app shell instead of the
  mockup's full-bleed focus mode (restyled, not redesigned).
